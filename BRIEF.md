## Revision 2 (user feedback, 2026-10-30)
"I liked the text animation, not the background. Make the background a drone that moves with you,
and remove the day/night mode."

- **New signature move: the flight.** A 3D quadcopter (three.js, built in code, no assets) flies the page
  with the reader over a contour map that streams underneath. It banks with direction, pitches with
  scroll speed, props spin faster when you scroll faster, and parks in empty space in each act.

- **Peak:** the drone flies straight at the viewer and passes, then "No cameras. No GPS." lands
  (true: his A2RL car navigated with neither), with the A2RL 1st place line under it.
- Close: contact links, drone hovering beside them.
